CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

CASA - Current Account Savings Account

CASA stands for Current Account Savings Account. It refers to the deposits maintained by customers in current accounts and savings accounts with a bank.

A savings account is generally used by customers to keep money safely while earning interest according to the bank's applicable interest rate.

A current account is generally designed for frequent transactions and business customers. Current accounts typically provide high transaction flexibility and may not provide interest in the same way as savings accounts.

KYC - Know Your Customer

KYC means Know Your Customer. Banks use KYC procedures to verify the identity and address of their customers.

Common KYC documents can include officially accepted identity documents and address proof. The exact documents required depend on applicable regulations and the bank's policies.

MFA - Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA means Multi-Factor Authentication. It is a security mechanism that requires a user to provide more than one type of authentication factor.

Examples include a password combined with a one-time password (OTP), authentication application approval, or another approved authentication factor.

PASSWORD RESET

If a customer forgets their banking application password, they can normally use the bank's approved password-reset process.

The customer may be required to verify their identity using registered contact information, OTP, MFA, or other security controls before creating a new password.

TRANSACTION ISSUE

For a transaction-related issue, customer support should first collect relevant transaction information and verify the customer's identity.

The support team should not request or expose sensitive authentication information such as passwords, PINs, or OTPs.

CUSTOMER DATA SECURITY

Customer passwords, PINs, OTPs, authentication tokens, and other confidential security credentials must not be disclosed to support personnel or other unauthorized parties.

Customer support responses should protect confidential information and follow the organization's security and privacy policies.
